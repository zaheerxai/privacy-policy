Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle information when you use our applications that integrate with Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and the Meta Graph API), including the Europe Mobile-Supplier Intelligence Engine and related tools.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Public business profile data obtained via Meta’s official Instagram Business Discovery API (e.g. username, biography, website, follower count, media count, and recent public post timestamps).
  • Public advertising data from the Meta Ad Library API (whether a business currently has active ads).
  • Technical data such as access tokens, app identifiers, and API request logs necessary to operate the integration.

We do not collect personal messages, private user content, or any data that is not publicly available through Meta’s official APIs.

2. How We Use the Information

The information is used solely for:

  • Business intelligence and supplier discovery purposes
  • Verifying that a business is active and publicly present on Instagram or running ads
  • Improving data quality and lead scoring in our systems

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the data for advertising targeting of individuals.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Data retrieved from Meta APIs may be stored in our databases for operational and analytical purposes. We retain this information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by applicable law.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share the collected data with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • With service providers who assist in operating our infrastructure (under confidentiality obligations)

5. Meta Platform Compliance

Our use of Meta APIs complies with Meta’s Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and the Instagram Platform Policy. We only access data that Meta makes available through official endpoints and with the appropriate permissions.

6. Your Rights

If you are a business whose public data has been processed and you wish to request access, correction, or deletion, please contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
