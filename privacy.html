Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Muhammad Zaheer / aimojo collects, uses, and handles information when using applications that integrate with Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and the Meta Graph API), including the Europe Mobile-Supplier Intelligence Engine and related tools.

1. Information We Collect

  • Public business profile data via Meta’s Instagram Business Discovery API (username, biography, website, follower count, media count, recent public post timestamps).
  • Public advertising data from the Meta Ad Library API (whether a business currently has active ads).
  • Technical data such as access tokens and API request logs necessary to operate the integration.

We do not collect personal messages, private user content, or any data that is not publicly available through Meta’s official APIs.

2. How We Use the Information

  • Business intelligence and supplier discovery
  • Verifying that a business is active on Instagram or running ads
  • Improving data quality and lead scoring

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising targeting of individuals.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Data retrieved from Meta APIs may be stored for operational and analytical purposes and is retained only as long as necessary or as required by law.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share collected data with third parties except when required by law or with service providers under confidentiality obligations.

5. Meta Platform Compliance

Our use of Meta APIs complies with Meta’s Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and Instagram Platform Policy.
